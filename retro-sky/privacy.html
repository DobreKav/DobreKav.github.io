Retro Sky Privacy Policy

Developer: NeonLabGames
Effective date: June 9, 2026

Data Collection

Retro Sky does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game has no account system, advertising, analytics, location tracking, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Local Settings

Sound and difficulty preferences are stored only on your device using Android local storage. These settings are not sent to NeonLabGames or any third party. They can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

Children's Privacy

Retro Sky does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because no personal data is collected, NeonLabGames retains no personal data. Local preferences are controlled by the user and deleted when app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled.

Security

Retro Sky is designed to work offline and requests no sensitive device permissions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when game features change. The effective date will be updated, and material changes will be reflected in the game and on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to neonlabgames@gmail.com.
