Retro Sky Privacy Policy

Developer: NeonLabGames
Effective date: June 9, 2026

Data Collection

NeonLabGames does not operate servers that collect personal information. Retro Sky uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Google may automatically process IP address, device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to provide, measure, secure, and personalize ads where consent permits.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for privacy choices before ads are requested. Available choices can be reopened from Settings under Ad Privacy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google advertising controls

Local Settings

Sound and difficulty preferences are stored only on your device using Android local storage. These settings are not sent to NeonLabGames or any third party. They can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the game.

Children's Privacy

Retro Sky is not directed to children under 13. NeonLabGames does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

NeonLabGames does not retain personal data on its own servers. Local preferences are deleted when app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Google controls retention and deletion for information processed by Google Mobile Ads under its privacy policy.

Security

Retro Sky requests internet and network-state access for advertising. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when game features change. The effective date will be updated, and material changes will be reflected in the game and on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to neonlabgames@gmail.com.
