PRIVACY POLICY

Your game, your choices.

Effective date: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravity Merge ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you use the Android application. Gravity Merge is an offline-first physics puzzle supported by advertising.

Information handled by the Game

Gravity Merge does not require an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, address, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment information. Scores, unlocked arenas, settings, and gameplay progress are stored locally on your device.

Advertising and consent

The Game uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, general device information, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize advertising where permitted.

Where required, the Game uses Google's consent tools before requesting personalized advertising. You may be offered non-personalized ads or consent controls depending on your region. Google's data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Sharing and saved images

Score sharing and score-card saving happen only when you choose those actions. Android's system share sheet controls which app receives the content. Saved score cards are written to a location you select or to app-managed storage according to Android permissions and platform behavior.

Device features

The Game may use vibration or haptic feedback and audio according to your settings. These features do not provide us with personal data.

Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear the application's storage or uninstall the Game. Advertising information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies and your Google account or advertising settings.

Children

Gravity Merge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The intended Google Play audience is ages 13 and older. If you believe a child has provided information through an advertising service, contact us using the method below.

Security and international processing

We limit the Game's own data handling to device-local progress. Third-party advertising providers may process information on servers in multiple countries and use safeguards described in their policies. No method of electronic processing is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

  • Use consent controls shown in the Game where available.
  • Change Android advertising and privacy settings.
  • Clear local progress through Android app-storage settings.
  • Uninstall the Game to remove its locally stored data.
  • Exercise regional privacy rights with Google for data it controls.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, advertising setup, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email displayed on the Gravity Merge Google Play listing. Include "Gravity Merge Privacy" in the subject line.
