Privacy Policy for Bubble War: Ages

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Bubble War: Ages is a puzzle game published by Neon Lab Games. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

Information we collect

The game does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location. Game progress, coins, and settings are stored locally on your device.

When advertising is enabled, our advertising partner Google AdMob may collect or receive device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, diagnostic information, and ad interaction data to provide, measure, secure, and personalize ads where permitted by law and your consent choices.

Advertising and consent

Bubble War: Ages may show banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose whether to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. We use Google AdMob to deliver ads. Google’s privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required, we will request consent before serving personalized advertising. You can change device-level advertising and privacy choices in your device settings.

How information is used

Information processed by our advertising partner may be used to display ads, limit fraud, measure advertising performance, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information directly.

Children

Bubble War: Ages is not directed to children under the age at which parental consent is required in their country. If the game is later directed to children or mixed audiences, we will update the advertising configuration and this policy before release.

Data retention and security

We retain no server-side game profiles. Local game data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the game. Google handles advertising data under its own retention and security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or applicable rules change. The newest version will always be posted at this address.
